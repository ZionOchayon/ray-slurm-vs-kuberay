# When to use which: Ray on Slurm or Ray on Kubernetes (KubeRay)

An opinion from this lab, v0.2. Every claim links either a row of [`results/results.csv`](results/results.csv) (measured by `make bench` on the host stated in the [README](../README.md#results)) or a source: external documentation, or an observation logged in [NOTES.md](NOTES.md#2026-10-27-v02). The lab runs on **one** machine, so it says nothing about hardware speed or scaling. It shows how each scheduler launches, recovers and shares a Ray job.

## Recommendation

- **Already run Slurm? Run Ray inside it.** One `sbatch` with `ray symmetric-run` starts the cluster, runs the driver on the head and tears it down, with no extra control plane ([Ray docs: Slurm](https://docs.ray.io/en/latest/cluster/vms/user-guides/community/slurm.html)). Submission to first training step took [24 s (median, n=5)](results/results.csv#L52) here, against [35 s (median, n=5)](results/results.csv#L49) for a KubeRay RayJob that creates its cluster per job.
- **Never let two `symmetric-run` jobs share nodes.** Each starts a Ray head on port 6379. With room on the nodes for both, at least one job failed in every rep, and Slurm still recorded jobs that never trained as `COMPLETED` ([both-failed 2/3, second-failed 1/3](results/results.csv#L116); [NOTES.md](NOTES.md#2026-10-27-v02)). Use `--exclusive`, which queues the second job ([serialized 3/3](results/results.csv#L117)), or per-job ports and temp dirs (`ray-manual.sbatch`), which let both run side by side ([both-concurrent 3/3](results/results.csv#L118)).
- **Check artifacts, not job states.** `symmetric-run` can exit 0 after `ray start` failed, so `COMPLETED` alone proves nothing. `make verify` reads `run.json`, the checkpoints, the job log and the `train.py` hash ([both-failed 2/3, second-failed 1/3](results/results.csv#L116); [NOTES.md](NOTES.md#2026-10-27-v02); [SPEC NFR-10](SPEC.md)).
- **Budget for `symmetric-run`'s tear-down on short jobs.** It holds the allocation about a minute after training ends, while the worker's raylet waits for the stopped head ([NOTES.md](NOTES.md#2026-10-27-v02)). Two short jobs took [234 s (median, n=3)](results/results.csv#L109) queued one after the other with `--exclusive`, against [43 s (median, n=3)](results/results.csv#L112) side by side with the manual launcher, which also releases its nodes within seconds.
- **Worker crashes are Ray's job on either scheduler.** With `FailureConfig(max_failures=3)`, Ray Train restarted the worker group and restored the last registered checkpoint on both ([pass 3/3](results/results.csv#L3); [pass 3/3](results/results.csv#L2)). Time to resume was [9.5 s (median, n=3)](results/results.csv#L100) on Slurm and [6.7 s (median, n=3)](results/results.csv#L97) on KubeRay ([Ray docs: fault tolerance](https://docs.ray.io/en/latest/train/user-guides/fault-tolerance.html)).
- **Preemption works on both, through different paths.** On Slurm, a USR1 trap plus `scontrol requeue` keeps the job ID and resumes from the checkpoint (admin-requeue: [pass 3/3](results/results.csv#L5); usr1-trap: [pass 3/3](results/results.csv#L6)), but the requeued job waits before it starts again (admin-requeue: [157 s (median, n=3)](results/results.csv#L28); usr1-trap: [180 s (median, n=3)](results/results.csv#L31); [`sbatch --signal`, `--requeue`](https://slurm.schedmd.com/sbatch.html)). On KubeRay, a deleted head pod keeps training through its grace period, then the job is resubmitted and resumes ([pass 3/3](results/results.csv#L4); [57 s (median, n=3)](results/results.csv#L25)).
- **Several jobs or teams on shared hardware: prefer KubeRay.** Each RayJob gets its own pods and network namespaces, so two jobs cannot collide on Ray ports; two RayJobs ran at the same time and both succeeded ([both-concurrent 3/3](results/results.csv#L115)). On Slurm, the safe options are exclusive nodes or hand-managed port blocks.
- **Elastic or long-lived Ray clusters: KubeRay.** The operator manages cluster lifecycle and retries as Kubernetes resources and supports autoscaling ([RayJob quick start](https://docs.ray.io/en/latest/cluster/kubernetes/getting-started/rayjob-quick-start.html)). A Slurm allocation is fixed for the life of the job.
- **Either way, put checkpoints on shared storage and trust only registered ones.** Multi-node Ray Train needs a path every worker can write ([Ray docs: persistent storage](https://docs.ray.io/en/latest/train/user-guides/persistent-storage.html)). A checkpoint on disk is restored only after Ray has registered it ([NOTES.md, D1](NOTES.md#2026-10-27-v02)).

## Decision table

| Factor | Ray on Slurm | Ray on Kubernetes (KubeRay) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing cluster type | Uses the Slurm cluster as is: `sbatch` + `ray symmetric-run` ([Ray docs](https://docs.ray.io/en/latest/cluster/vms/user-guides/community/slurm.html)); first step after [24 s (median, n=5)](results/results.csv#L52) | Needs Kubernetes, the KubeRay operator and an image with your code ([RayJob quick start](https://docs.ray.io/en/latest/cluster/kubernetes/getting-started/rayjob-quick-start.html)); first step after [35 s (median, n=5)](results/results.csv#L49) |
| Multi-tenancy | Two `symmetric-run` jobs on the same nodes break each other ([both-failed 2/3, second-failed 1/3](results/results.csv#L116)); use `--exclusive` ([serialized 3/3](results/results.csv#L117)) or per-job ports ([both-concurrent 3/3](results/results.csv#L118)) | Pods isolate jobs; two RayJobs ran at once ([both-concurrent 3/3](results/results.csv#L115)) |
| Preemption model | Signal, trap, requeue; same job ID, resume from checkpoint (admin-requeue: [pass 3/3](results/results.csv#L5); usr1-trap: [pass 3/3](results/results.csv#L6); [sbatch](https://slurm.schedmd.com/sbatch.html), [scontrol](https://slurm.schedmd.com/scontrol.html)) | Pod deletion with a grace period, then resubmission and resume ([pass 3/3](results/results.csv#L4); [Kubernetes preemption](https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/scheduling-eviction/pod-priority-preemption/)) |
| Elasticity | Fixed allocation per job ([sbatch](https://slurm.schedmd.com/sbatch.html)) | Operator-managed clusters with autoscaling available ([RayJob quick start](https://docs.ray.io/en/latest/cluster/kubernetes/getting-started/rayjob-quick-start.html)) |
| Operational surface | The Slurm daemons you already run; job scripts are bash ([Ray docs](https://docs.ray.io/en/latest/cluster/vms/user-guides/community/slurm.html)) | Operator, CRDs, images and a registry or `kind load` ([RayJob quick start](https://docs.ray.io/en/latest/cluster/kubernetes/getting-started/rayjob-quick-start.html)) |
| Fit for the team's skills | HPC teams fluent in `sbatch`, `srun` and `sacct` ([Slurm docs](https://slurm.schedmd.com/sbatch.html)) | Platform teams fluent in manifests, Helm and `kubectl` ([KubeRay docs](https://docs.ray.io/en/latest/cluster/kubernetes/getting-started/rayjob-quick-start.html)) |
